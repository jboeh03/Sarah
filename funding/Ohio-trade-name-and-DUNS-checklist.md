# Do-It-Today Checklist — Register in Ohio + Start Your Business Credit File

**For:** Tri-State Grill Cleaning / Cincinnati Grill Cleaning (Jeff Boeh, Cincinnati OH)
**Why:** These two cheap steps make the business "real" to banks, let you keep accounts in
the business name, and open a **business** credit file so future credit stops leaning on
your personal score. Total cost: **$39** (one name) and **$0** for D-U-N-S.

> Note: you currently operate under **two** names — "Tri-State Grill Cleaning" (your Mercury
> account name) and "Cincinnati Grill Cleaning" (your website/email). Pick the one you
> **invoice and bank under** as the primary registration. Registering both = $39 each.

---

## Part 1 — Register your trade name with the Ohio Secretary of State ($39)

**What it is:** Ohio **Form 534A, "Name Registration."** As a sole prop using a name other
than your own, this is the filing that lets you (and many banks/vendors) operate under the
business name. Choose **"Trade Name"** (not "Fictitious Name") — a trade name gives you
**exclusive rights** to it; a fictitious name does not. ([ORC 1329.01][orc], [Form 534A][form])

**Steps:**
1. **Check the name is available first.** Search the Ohio business name database so your
   filing isn't rejected for being too similar to an existing one.
   → Ohio Business Search: <https://businesssearch.ohiosos.gov/>
2. **File Form 534A online** through **Ohio Business Central** (create a free login):
   → <https://bsportal.sos.state.oh.us/> (or start at <https://www.ohiosos.gov/businesses/>)
   - Select **Name Registration → Trade Name**.
   - **Name to register:** `Tri-State Grill Cleaning` (and/or `Cincinnati Grill Cleaning`).
   - **Date first used in business:** put the real first-use date (e.g., when you opened the
     Mercury account or first advertised). It must be **before** today.
   - **Registrant:** your legal name (sole proprietor) + business address.
3. **Pay the $39 fee.** Standard processing is a few business days. Expedite tiers exist
   (~$100 two-day / $200 one-day / $300 four-hour walk-in) if you're in a hurry — **not
   needed** for this. ([Ohio SOS fee schedule][form])
4. **Save the stamped confirmation** (PDF). You'll use it to keep Mercury/PayPal and vendor
   accounts in the business name.

**Time:** ~15 minutes to file. ✅ when you have the confirmation number.

## Part 2 — Get your free D-U-N-S number (starts your business credit file)

**What it is:** A free Dun & Bradstreet ID that opens your **business** credit file (the
D&B "PAYDEX" score). Net-30 vendors and the Mercury IO card report here — this is the file
they build. ([D&B — Get a free D-U-N-S][duns], [NerdWallet][nw])

**Steps:**
1. Go to the **official free** page (don't pay a third party):
   → <https://www.dnb.com/en-us/smb/duns/get-a-duns.html>
2. Enter the business **exactly** as registered: legal name, the **trade name** from Part 1,
   address, phone, owner name, structure (sole prop), industry, employee count.
3. Submit. **Free processing takes up to ~30 business days** (often faster). Skip the $229
   expedite — you don't need the speed.

**Time:** ~10 minutes. ✅ when you receive your 9-digit D-U-N-S number by email.

## Part 3 — Lock in consistency (the part everyone skips)

Business credit only builds if your identity is **identical everywhere**. Pick one exact
spelling/format of the name + address and use it on **all** of these:

- [ ] Mercury account name
- [ ] PayPal **Business** account name
- [ ] D-U-N-S registration
- [ ] Any net-30 vendor accounts (incl. The BBQ Depot)
- [ ] Invoices

Mismatches ("Tri-State Grill Cleaning" vs "Tri State Grill Cleaning LLC" vs "Cincinnati
Grill Cleaning") split your credit history across multiple files and slow everything down.

Once Parts 1–3 are done you can, in order:

   reports to your new D&B file).
2. **Open 2–3 reporting net-30 vendor accounts** for supplies you already buy.
3. **Check your PayPal Working Capital offer** (based on PayPal sales, no credit check).
